We Can Detect These Skin Conditions

Melanoma

A type of skin cancer that develops from the pigment-producing cells known as melanocytes.

Psoriasis

A chronic autoimmune skin condition that causes red, scaly patches on the skin.

Eczema

A condition that makes the skin red, inflamed, and itchy.

Basal Cell Carcinoma

A type of skin cancer that begins in the basal cells and appears as a slightly transparent bump.

Rosacea

A chronic skin condition that causes redness and visible blood vessels in the face.

Vitiligo

A condition in which the skin loses its pigment cells, leading to discolored patches.

Actinic Keratosis

A rough, scaly patch on the skin that develops from years of exposure to the sun.

Tinea

A fungal infection that can affect different parts of the body, also known as ringworm.

Hives

A skin rash triggered by allergens, appearing as red, raised, itchy welts.
